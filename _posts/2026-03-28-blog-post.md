Great to see so much growing attention to environmental exposure risks at #AAG2026 this year! 

I was excited to present our research: **"A Spatiotemporal Analysis of Intersecting Social and Environmental Vulnerabilities in Columbus, Ohio"** (co-authored with Sandy Wong, Allan Just, and Huyen Le). 

I also had the chance to meet and learn from many inspiring women scholars working in this area. Their work and perspectives gave me a lot to think about, and I’m grateful to be part of such a thoughtful and supportive research community.
