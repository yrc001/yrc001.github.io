The **Chicago Travel Study** is a research project conducted by our research team at The Ohio State University, in collaboration with **Dr. Andre Carrel** and **Dr. Huyen Le**. 

This research focuses on conducting transit surveys in Chicago to examine public transit experiences, environmental exposures, and individual travel well-being.

### Participant Recruitment
We are currently recruiting participants! If you live, work, or travel in the Chicago area, we warmly invite you to join our study and share your transit experiences.

For more details and to participate, please visit our project website: [Chicago Travel Study](https://u.osu.edu/chicagotravelstudy/)
